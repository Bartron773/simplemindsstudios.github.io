Editorial Principles

Wonder, with standards.

Simple Minds Studios

The studio is not organized around a single medium. It is organized around a way of thinking: notice what is difficult to understand, stay curious long enough to see its structure, and make that structure visible.

We'd rather be curious than certain. That's a more disciplined kind of attention.

The working principle

Making things make sense.

Artificial intelligence is used as a cognitive and creative tool: to challenge assumptions, clarify thought, connect ideas, prototype possibilities, and expand what one person can investigate and build.

Selected projects may include credited collaborators, technical partners, and contributions from multiple AI systems.

How we got here

From curiosity to architecture.

We began with Thinking Clearer as a podcast. Then a podcast with a website. Then connected to research papers, photography, museum ideas, AI collaboration. For a while they felt like separate threads. The Spatial Habitat showed us they were part of the same question. A thinking environment. Instead of organizing information, it organizes relationships. Instead of rewarding speed, it encourages attention. The podcast became the doorway. The studio became a place that designs thinking environments. Each project is a different habitat asking the same oldest question: What are we actually doing when we think? AI is the newest mirror.

Start a conversation

Bring the difficult thing.

Some conversations become collaborations. Others become friendships. Some last fifteen minutes. Some last for years.

Simple Minds Studios exists because people are willing to stay with difficult questions a little longer than most.

If you're curious about AI, have a project that needs another perspective, want to commission new work, need someone to speak or contribute to your publication, or simply want to have an honest conversation about an idea, you're welcome here.

There is no expectation that you already know what you're looking for.

Sometimes the conversation is the project.
