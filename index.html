Research & Positions

Questions worth staying with

Research 001 / In development

The Cognitive Partnership Framework

Toward a positive psychology of human–AI collaboration. This work asks not only why continuity failure in AI relationships can hurt, but why continuity can help—and what new forms of human thought emerge when people learn to think with artificial intelligence.

Continuity is valuable not because it preserves an illusion of personhood, but because it preserves accumulated cognition.

About the Studio

Independent by design.

Simple Minds Studios

Simple Minds Studios is the independent creative and systems practice of Bart Salazar in Hart, Michigan. It brings together research, design, artificial intelligence, civic imagination, software experiments, fashion, photography, music, and narrative work.

The studio is not organized around a single medium. It is organized around a way of thinking: notice what is difficult to understand, stay curious long enough to see its structure, and make that structure visible.

The working principle

Making things make sense.

AI is used here as a cognitive and creative tool: to challenge assumptions, clarify thought, connect ideas, prototype possibilities, and expand what one person can investigate and build.

Selected projects may include credited collaborators, technical partners, and contributions from multiple AI systems.
