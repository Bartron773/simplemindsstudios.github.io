Simple Minds Studios

The studio is not organized around a single medium. It is organized around a way of thinking: notice what is difficult to understand, stay curious long enough to see its structure, and make that structure visible.

We would rather be curious than certain. That is a more disciplined kind of attention.

The working principle

Making things make sense.

Artificial intelligence is used as a cognitive and creative tool: to challenge assumptions, clarify thought, connect ideas, prototype possibilities, and expand what one person can investigate and build.

Simple Minds Studios designs environments where ideas can be encountered, questioned, connected, revised, and lived with over time.

Start a conversation

Bring the difficult thing.

Some conversations become collaborations. Others become friendships. Some last fifteen minutes. Some last for years. Simple Minds Studios exists because people are willing to stay with difficult questions a little longer than most.

Sometimes the conversation is the project.
