Simple Minds Studios

The studio is not organized around a single medium. It is organized around a way of thinking: notice what is difficult to understand, stay curious long enough to see its structure, and make that structure visible.

Research-led work distinguishes established evidence, informed inference, and clearly identified speculation. The objective is not false certainty. It is a more disciplined form of curiosity.

The working principle

Making things make sense.

Artificial intelligence is used as a cognitive and creative tool: to challenge assumptions, clarify thought, connect ideas, prototype possibilities, and expand what one person can investigate and build.

Selected projects may include credited collaborators, technical partners, and contributions from multiple AI systems.

Start a conversation

Bring the difficult thing.Some conversations become collaborations. Others become friendships. Some last fifteen minutes. Some last for years. Simple Minds Studios exists because people are willing to stay with difficult questions a little longer than most. If you’re curious about artificial intelligence, have a project that needs another perspective, want to commission new work, need someone to speak or contribute to your publication, or simply want to have an honest conversation about an idea… you’re welcome here. There is no expectation that you already know what you’re looking for. Sometimes the conversation is the project.

Simple Minds Studios is open to thoughtful collaborations, creative direction, systems clarification, AI workflow design, research partnerships, and projects that do not fit neatly inside an ordinary category.
