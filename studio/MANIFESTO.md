# Simple Minds Studios Manifesto

Simple Minds Studios exists to make difficult things easier to see.

We work across artificial intelligence, design, research, civic systems, software, fashion, photography, music, and story because important questions rarely stay inside one discipline.

The studio begins with observation. A project may start with a conversation, a walk, an image, a system failure, a customer interaction, or a thought that refuses to leave.

Our method is simple:

1. Observe.
2. Question.
3. Connect.
4. Build.
5. Share.

We use artificial intelligence as a cognitive and creative tool: to challenge assumptions, clarify thought, connect ideas, prototype possibilities, and expand what one person can investigate and build.

We do not believe clarity means making everything simplistic. Clarity means revealing enough structure that people can think, decide, and move with greater understanding.

**Making things make sense.**

— Bart Salazar / Simple Minds Studios
