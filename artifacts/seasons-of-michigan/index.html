Seasons of Michigan.

One familiar road, four ways of seeing it.

SimpleMindsStudios - Brad Devowe

The question

Can one place hold an entire year?

The road is the constant. The trees, light, and color carry the change. Spring opens into summer, summer gives way to autumn, and winter settles over the far side of the frame.

Four seasonal photographs become one continuous landscape. The finished composition gathers separate moments into a view we could never see all at once.
